About Sign2Speech

🎯 Our Mission

Sign2Speech is an innovative application that bridges the communication gap between sign language and spoken language. Using advanced machine learning and neural networks, we convert American Sign Language (ASL) gestures into real-time speech, making communication more accessible for everyone.

🚀 Technology

Our application leverages cutting-edge technologies including:

  • MediaPipe for hand tracking and gesture recognition
  • TensorFlow.js for neural network-based classification
  • Web Speech API for text-to-speech conversion
  • Real-time video processing and feature extraction

✨ Features

  • Real-time ASL alphabet recognition
  • Custom sign training and model creation
  • Word and sentence building
  • Multiple voice options for speech synthesis
  • Excel data import for bulk training
  • Model export and sharing capabilities

👥 Who We Are

We are a team of developers passionate about making technology accessible and inclusive. Our goal is to create tools that help break down communication barriers and empower the deaf and hard-of-hearing community.
